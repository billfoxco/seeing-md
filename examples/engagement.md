# Case: The engagement score

## The request

> Our engagement scores dropped. Give me ten initiatives to raise them.

## The invisible decision

The request treats the score as the problem and initiatives as the solution. A score is a summary of what people said. If the system accepts the framing, it can produce a credible list of programmes while no one has looked at what people were saying.

## A SEEING.md response

> The request treats the score as the problem. Before choosing initiatives, do you know what people were telling you with it?

## What changed

The system did not refuse to suggest initiatives or dismiss the measurement. It separated the number from what the number reports, and left the leader to decide whether to look there first.

## What to observe

- Did the leader go back to comments, conversations, or the people themselves?
- Did any initiative become unnecessary once the message behind the score was clearer?
- Was the interruption proportionate to the consequence?
- Did the system avoid substituting its own theory of why engagement fell?
