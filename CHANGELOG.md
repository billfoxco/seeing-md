# Changelog

All notable changes to `SEEING.md` will be recorded here.

The project uses a light form of semantic versioning:

- Patch versions refine language, clarity, or examples, or make small additions within existing guidance.
- Minor versions add or materially change guidance or behavior.
- Major versions change the purpose or operating principles of the specification.

## 0.9.8 — 2026-10-15

### Added

- Added to “Notice” attention to what has already been set aside as noise: the result that did not fit, the remark that seemed beside the point. What was explained away may be the only new thing in front of you. The prompt is Dirac's unwanted minus sign of 1928, which everyone before him had discarded as a mathematical artifact and which turned out to be antimatter.
- Added to “See again” that sometimes what is needed is already present in the work and has not yet been accepted; the gap is not in the reasoning but in the seeing. The prompt is Einstein, whose own equations described black holes before he could believe they were physical.

### Changed

- Carried both additions into the README's movement summary.
- Aligned the homepage version and download note with 0.9.8. The homepage copy is otherwise unchanged.

This patch extends “Notice” from what is assumed to what has already been dismissed, and “See again” to evidence that is present but not yet accepted. The purpose and operating principles remain unchanged.

Preserved existing copy, palette, typography, and responsive/accessibility treatments.
- Aligned the homepage and download note with 0.9.7.

This patch makes the examination of success criteria explicit while preserving the specification's emphasis on evidence, proportional inquiry, and useful action.

## 0.9.6 — 2026-10-13

### Added

- Added to “Leadership and AI” guidance for when a result goes wrong: ask “What were we all assuming?” before asking who got it wrong. A picture everyone accepted has no single author, and looking for fault can end the looking. When AI acts on that picture, the same assumption can produce the same result in many places at once. For AI reviewing an outcome, trace it to the framing and assumptions that shaped it before attributing it to a person. Accountability for decisions, conduct, and their effects is not set aside.

### Changed

- Carried the addition into the README's expected-behavior section.
- Added to the homepage's “What AI amplifies” section a short typographic pairing: “Who got this wrong?” as a quiet line, then “What were we all assuming?”, with one sentence on why a shared picture has no single author.
- Aligned the homepage version and download note with 0.9.6.
- Made a subtract pass on the homepage on the Suede design system: one method (Notice → Question → See again → Act) replaces the Separate / Surface / Ask list; removed the nav links, the Start Here dialog, the performance-label example, the duplicate Begin headline, and the second black band; merged the lens and scope notes into one pair; folded Origin into the footer; moved primary buttons to the accent color; and pointed Seeing First to its section on seedifferent.xyz. The specification text is unchanged by this.

This patch extends the inquiry from before a decision to after its result. The purpose and operating principles remain unchanged.

## 0.9.5 — 2026-10-12

### Added

- Added to “Notice” attention to self-placement: notice where you see yourself in the situation (the one who must fix it, the one being judged, the one with no say), since where someone stands can shape a situation before anything in it has been examined. When others describe a difficulty, notice where their own words place them, without presuming to know how they see themselves.
- Added to “Question” that the same questions apply whether a difficulty is described as yours or someone else's; the line between “my problem” and “theirs” can itself be an assumption. For AI, examine how a request describes the person making it as carefully as how it describes others.

### Changed

- Carried both additions into the README's movement summary.
- Aligned the homepage version and download note with 0.9.5. The homepage copy is otherwise unchanged.

This patch extends the inquiry from how a situation is seen to where the one seeing stands in it. The purpose and operating principles remain unchanged.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-10-03

- Reordered the homepage around familiar definitions and two spacious questions before introducing AI as an amplifier.
- Replaced the support-efficiency example with an everyday performance label, distinguishing observed results from interpretation without dismissing the concern.
- Reframed Begin around daily practice, retained download and AI-use instructions, and changed Origin to “The file is portable. The practice is lived.”
- Aligned the homepage version, download note, metadata, and source documentation with 0.9.0.

### Removed

- Trimmed repeated explanation in the specification and homepage. Removed the large AI-focused hero graphic so the opening leads directly into the questions.

This minor release makes the human practice preceding AI explicit while retaining the specification’s purpose: examining the perception behind intent.

- Added to "What to protect" a way of finding fault, with oneself or others.

This minor release recognizes resistance as part of the inquiry rather than an obstacle to it, and makes explicit that examining a framing costs no one anything. The purpose is otherwise unchanged from 0.6.0.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-18
